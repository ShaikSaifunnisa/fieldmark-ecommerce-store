Our Story

We started FIELDMARK because most gear wasn't built to be fixed.

FIELDMARK began in 2019 with one backpack and a broken zipper. Rather than replace it, our founder took it apart, rebuilt it, and asked why more outdoor gear wasn't designed to survive that kind of repair in the first place. Every product we've made since has been built the same way: simple construction, replaceable parts, and materials chosen to hold up to actual trail use — not just a showroom shelf.

Built to be repaired

Every zipper, buckle and strap on a FIELDMARK product is replaceable. We publish repair guides for everything we sell.

Tested where it matters

New designs spend a season in the hands of trail crews before they ever reach the shop — not a lab, the actual route.

Smaller runs, less waste

We produce in smaller batches and reuse offcut material in accessories, rather than chasing constant new drops.

"A pack you can take apart with one hand and a hex key is a pack that lasts a decade."
— Founder's note, 2019
